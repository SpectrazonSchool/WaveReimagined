# Wave Reimagined

**Wave Reimagined** turns the wave gamemode's trail into a **cooler looking trail**.

The trail becomes a solid white blade with no blending, wrapped in a coloured
glow that fades from your wave trail's colour out to transparent. The blade can
pulse with the beat and the glow can flare with the music.

Everything is tunable in the mod's settings:

- **Blade thickness** and a **music pulse** that thickens the blade on the beat.
- **Glow size**, **glow strength**, and **additive glow** for a brighter neon look.
- **Music-reactive glow** that flares with the music on top of its normal strength.
- A **fix wave drift** physics toggle (off by default; not for legitimate top-play
  submissions) and a master on/off switch.
- **Draw fix** for the wave trail so everything is perfectly clean and symmetrical 

If you want your wave trail to not be stupid when it slides off of D blocks, download [Wave Trail Drag Fix](https://geode-sdk.org/mods/nytelyte.wave_trail_drag_fix)
