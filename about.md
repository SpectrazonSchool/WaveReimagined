# Wave Reimagined

**Wave Reimagined** turns the wave gamemode's trail into a **lightsaber**.

The trail becomes a solid white blade with no blending, wrapped in a coloured
glow that fades from your wave trail's colour out to transparent. The blade
also stops pulsing to the music, so it stays a clean, constant width.

Everything is tunable in the mod's settings: glow size, glow strength,
additive vs. flat glow, the no-pulse toggle, and a master on/off switch.
